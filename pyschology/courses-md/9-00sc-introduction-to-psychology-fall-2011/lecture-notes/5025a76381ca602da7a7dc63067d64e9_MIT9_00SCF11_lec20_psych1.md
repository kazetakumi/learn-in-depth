## Treatment

- neuroleptics
- -block post-synaptic dopamine receptors &amp; release of dopamine from presynaptic neurons
- drug is fully effective at receptors within hours but maximal clinical effect takes weeks and remain for weeks after treatment ends
- side effects
- -early on - parkinsonian symptoms (20-40%)
- -later on - tardive dyskinesia (20%)
- -abnormal involuntary movements - smacking lips, chewing, tongue protrusion
- -clozapine (late 1980s) - no tardive dyskinesia, but other risks including live function, reduction in white cells, must be monitored, expensive
- -behavioral therapy can enhance drug efficacy
